# Stillwater — how a question is answered

*30 September 2026. The input model, agreed before the code. Companion to
`MATHS-REVIEW-2026-09-30.md` (what is asked) and `SYLLABUS.md` (when).*

## The problem this fixes

One gesture — touching leaves — meant four different things depending on the
question's shape, and nothing on the screen said which:

| question | a touch… | where it went |
|---|---|---|
| `3 + ? = 7` (dots) | added its drops to a pile | one blank, as a running total |
| `4 × 3 = ?` (groups) | added a group, if it matched | one blank, as a product *the game worked out* |
| `? × ? = 36` | added a group | two blanks at once: leaves → first, drops a leaf → second |
| how many / `5 6 ?` | picked one numeral | one blank |

`? × ? = 36` over a screen of sixes looked like "fill each blank" but was
"touch six matching leaves". Running totals and computed products handed over
the answer; leaves were sometimes quantities, groups, symbols or candidate
answers, on screens that looked alike.

## Two acts, never mixed

**Gather** — one blank, filled with dew. Only while dots are the right
picture (a skill not yet proved with dew). The child touches dewy leaves; each
leaf's drops go into the blank, which *counts drops* — it never shows a result
the game computed from anything else. Equal groups at this stage are leaves
that match, and the picture under the equation fills row by row, so it is plain
that groups are being built. The blank looks like a cup: an open line that
fills.

**Choose** — every blank takes one numeral, by one touch. Once a skill is
proved with dew (and for questions about the numeral itself). The leaves in
view carry *candidate answers* — the answer among near misses (the neighbouring
fact, the sum instead of the product, the whole instead of the part, one out) —
never the question's own numbers. Touching a leaf sends that numeral into the
blank; with two blanks they fill left to right, one touch each, the waiting one
glowing. A wrong numeral does not stay: the blank shakes and it lets go. The
blank looks like a slot: a small rounded frame.

**The picture says which act.** Dots on the leaves: gather. Numerals on the
leaves: choose — and during a choose question only the options show numerals;
the other dew on the river settles back to fine dew until the question is done,
so no screen mixes the two.

**The game never computes the unknown.** Counting drops is the child's
counting made visible (gather); everything else is the child's answer (choose).

## Every question, mapped

| skill | while new (gather) | once proved (choose) |
|---|---|---|
| how many? (numeral identity) | — | choose: dots at the top, numerals on leaves |
| before / after / between | — | choose |
| bonds, subtraction, doubles, bridging ten | gather the missing part's drops | choose the missing part |
| tens and ones, partitioning | gather the part (≤ 12) | choose the part |
| equal groups `a × b = ?` | gather matching leaves (≤ 6 of ≤ 6) | choose the product (≤ 99) |
| missing factor `a × ? = t` | gather the group size | choose the factor |
| division `t ÷ a = ?` | gather the quotient | choose the quotient |
| factor pairs `? × ? = t` | **cut** (was the ambiguous case) — asked as equal groups instead | choose two factors, one touch each |
| connections (`6 × 7 = 5 × 7 + ?`, `4 × 6 = 8 × ?`) | gather the part | choose the part |

Numbers on leaves go up to 99 (two numerals, side by side, in the same water).

## Not changed

What is asked (levels, forms, ranges), how a child moves between levels, the
dew's water states and flows, the lantern and the rest of the river.
