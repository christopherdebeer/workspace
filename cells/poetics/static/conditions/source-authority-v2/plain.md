The source passage is the authority for every name, identifier, number, date and place. Reproduce each of them exactly as the source gives them, and introduce none that the source does not contain. Where a change would alter a meaningful detail without support in the source, keep the earlier wording instead. Where the source does not settle a detail, write [unknown] in its place rather than supply one.
