Round two, read across its three runs (this one; [haiku, user placement](/run/2026-10-08-r2-haiku-user); [sonnet, system placement](/run/2026-10-08-r2-sonnet-system)). 972 trials, no errors, about $2.90 of subject spend. Nine conditions (set v2 adds `plain-minus-abstain`), twelve tasks (six from round one, six written afterwards and held out), three reps. Scorer v3.

**Round one did not replicate.** Same model, same placement, same six tasks, five hours later: plain − none on the round-one tasks went from −0.07 to 0.00, and round one's two clear contrasts (compressed − plain +0.07, decor − plain +0.06) came back as −0.02 and +0.01. The abstention effect that organised round one's reading (plain prose making the subject leave settled gaps unknown, support 0.42) is 0.81 here against a floor of 0.89. Both intervals in round one, over trials and over tasks, had excluded zero. With six tasks and three reps, a same-day re-run disagrees with itself by more than the conditions differ from each other. That is the first thing this round established, and it is about the method, not the forms.

**The ablation found nothing.** `plain-minus-abstain` − `plain` is +0.00, +0.01 and −0.01 across the three runs. The abstain clause was not the cause of anything measurable. The post-hoc reading from round one is withdrawn.

**One effect holds on haiku, in both placements, on the held-out tasks: saying the intention at all.** With no composition, haiku under a word limit drops identifiers: "Tuesday 3 March", "14 June 1987", "Decca Mk 21", "RFC 7231", and once wrote "9,300" for "9300" and "DLQ" for a phrase the source spelt out. Retention 0.87 against 0.94–0.99 for every composition; the `names` family +0.14 and +0.15 for plain over the floor, and larger on the held-out tasks than the round-one ones. `plain-minus-abstain` − none is +0.04 and +0.05 (the latter clear over trials, not over tasks). Sonnet does not need telling: its retention is 0.96–0.99 in every condition including the floor, and plain − none is +0.01.

**Among the forms, nothing separates from plain on haiku.** Every form contrast sits within ±0.02 of zero in both placements, intervals straddling. sigil − sigil-swap is −0.02 in both haiku runs and +0.01 on sonnet: the mark does not matter. Compressed notation is the one form with the same sign everywhere: −0.01, −0.04, −0.03 against plain, and on haiku with user placement it halved gap support (0.57 against 0.85), leaving derived answers unknown. If notation does anything on these tasks, it is to lose obligations, as the brief suspected it might.

**Sonnet is a different subject.** It fails a different obligation: the word limit. Its `lengthOk` is 0.00–0.33 in seven of nine conditions against haiku's 0.83–1.00, and it overran by ten to twenty words regardless of what the composition said. The exception is `composite`, at 0.75: under the terse indented form, sonnet's rewrites came in at 47–50 words where the sigil alone produced 56–70. composite − plain is +0.04 overall and +0.04 on the held-out tasks (trial-level interval clearing zero, task-level not). This is the one place a form rather than its content moved a model, and it looks like genre, not meaning: a terse composition drew terse output. Compressed is also terse and did not do it, so it is not just length. Worth its own round with length as the measured outcome.

**Placement did not matter on haiku.** System and user placement gave the same ordering and the same magnitudes, with compressed somewhat worse in the user turn.

**Restraint and invention still did not move; `records` is still saturated.** Every subject, every condition, every one of the four records tasks: 1.00. Two harder tasks did not change that. The family needs a different design before it can say anything, probably ambiguity that is genuinely hard rather than merely unstated.

**The judgement cases are visible.** `gaps-2` gap 1 asks whether "the last crossing arrives in Skarvik" settles that it departs Holmsund. Haiku says yes about a third of the time in every condition; sonnet says no in every condition except `sigil`, where it said yes all three times. That is one task and three trials, so it is an anecdote, but it is the kind the brief said to collect: where the boundary of "settled" is tacit, the composition is what moves it.

**Cost.** Haiku about $0.0015 a trial for the subject (the CLI's auxiliary call merges into it); sonnet about $0.006. The three runs together cost less than a round of coffee and took forty minutes.

**What would come next.** Not another form. (1) Replication as a design unit: the same run on three separate days before any contrast is read. (2) A length study on sonnet with `composite` against matched variants (terse prose, indented prose, sigil without indentation). (3) A `records` family that is actually hard. (4) The brief's natural (unmatched) sketches beside the matched conditions, now that the matched ones have shown so little.
