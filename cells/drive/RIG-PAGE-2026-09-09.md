# Rig page, paint, fittings and registration

Ranger roof and utility-body material now share the selected paint colour object. Its wear amounts remain distinct, and glazing/trim/equipment retain material colours. Cab ghosting recognises all surfaces sharing the paint colour.

client/rig-equipment.ts owns 13 independent visual fittings for both models: rack, solar, tent, awning, crates, cans, spare, ladder, snorkel, winch, boards, mast and lights. Classic's previously permanent expedition accessories and Ranger's preset equipment blocks were removed from the base builders. The equipment geometry is merged by material to bound draw calls. Each fitting includes its own required mounting hardware. Physical handling and energy simulation are unchanged. drive.rig-model now stores equipment alongside model/loadout; old preset-only saves migrate through equipmentFor. An empty array means none, not default.

Rig opens on the live world, with an orbiting vehicle above a scrolling fitting sheet. Custom paint is near the top; None/All shortcuts and native checkbox labels support individual selection without rebuilding the DOM or losing focus. Studio/elevations are retained behind STUDIO / VIEWS. Dimensions/specification are folded into a disclosure, and live dimensions refresh after changing fittings. The live rig camera is allowed even when entering from chart, drone or GPS mode; those underlying modes are not changed.

Front/rear plates and the Rig-page plate badge show a sanitised uppercase username (12 characters maximum), or DRIVE-01. setRegistration updates pixel-glyph geometry only when identity changes; the host checks once per second and rebuilds the derived silhouette. No canvas texture or external font request is required.

Validation: rig/menu TypeScript checks and main syntax parsing passed. Thirty configurations (bare, each individual item, all, on both models) have finite geometry; full-body Ranger custom paint and username/default changes pass. Twelve swaps preserve physical position, custom colour and wheel pose. DOM checks pass for thirteen checkboxes, None/All, model changes retaining fittings, live/studio controls and existing menu actions. Full kit height is 3.27m Classic / 3.77m Ranger versus bare 1.99m / 2.46m, including wheels. The initial test's arbitrary 1.5x vertex-count criterion was replaced by added-geometry and silhouette-height checks: Classic's existing high-tessellation arches make a ratio misleading. Real WebGL appearance and mobile FPS remain unverified in this environment.
