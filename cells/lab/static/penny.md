# Penny Vision

An optical amusement from Auxiliary Field. Admission one imaginary penny. Wear anaglyph glasses with the red filter over the left eye and choose the colour of the other lens. Reversed pairs have their own setting.

## At the glass

Select an engraved plate. Turn it with a sideways drag, or focus the specimen canvas and use the arrow keys. Drag vertically or use Focus to move the plane where both images coincide. Follow a cut across that plane and its coloured edges change sides. Relief sets the eye separation. Start low and turn it up to taste. Monocular proof pulls a single greyscale impression.

## Three plates

The Trefoil ties one continuous length into a knot. The Armillary nests four independently tilted hoops. The Volute winds out and back through its own centre. Each carries a ruled maker’s band. The marks follow the surface as it turns.

Both eyes see the same engraved mesh from parallel, horizontally displaced viewpoints. Each pass clears its own depth buffer, so an edge can hide a different piece of the specimen in each eye. An off-axis projection registers the chosen focus distance. The red and cyan channels carry independent greyscale impressions. Red / blue sends the second impression to blue alone. The frame stays registered at the surface of the glass.
