# Round four: an oil sketch (23 November)

`slight-dive-sketch.jpg`: the slight figure's dive as an alla prima oil sketch on a toned board. Faithful to the character (lavender
top, dark trousers, white shoes, brown ponytail, ochre scarf). Much closer to what a stroke renderer is, than round three's
finished painting:

- **A toned ground.** Warm ochre board everywhere, showing between and through the strokes, warm against the cool greys and
  lavenders. It holds the picture together.
- **Few, big, curved strokes that follow the gesture.**
  - The trousers are four or five broad strokes bent along the legs: dark violet beneath, a lighter one over the top of the
    thigh.
  - The sweater is six or eight strokes along the arm and back, lit lilac laid over the mid-tone.
  - The shoes are two or three white impasto strokes; the hair a few brown strokes and one calligraphic flick (the ponytail's
    arc); the face a single shadow stroke; the hand a couple of light touches; the scarf two ochre strokes.
- **The brush shows.** Loaded paint with bristle texture, impasto on the lights, dry-brush breaking up at the ends where the brush
  runs out. Edges are broken, never ruled.
- **The setting is the same strokes.** Horizontal for the floor, vertical for the wall, big and loose, with the ground showing.
  The cast shadow is one dark horizontal drag.
- **Economy with conviction.** Each stroke is varied (width, pressure, curve, load), so a few say a lot.
