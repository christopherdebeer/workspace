# Round two: single-frame studies (22 November)

Image-generation studies made on the clean study frames (`devtools/roofjumps-studies.mjs`, after G1-G3), one frame each:
- `slight-drive.jpg`: the slight figure's drive, from the side;
- `older-vault.jpg`: the older figure's vault;
- `child-hang.jpg`: the child's hang;
- `schoolgirl-flight.jpg`: the schoolgirl in flight, from behind;
- `teen-stand-front.jpg`: the teen standing, from the front;
- `runner-stand-back.jpg`: the runner standing, from behind;
- `broad-takeoff.jpg`: the broad figure's takeoff.

They agree with one another, so they read as a direction, not a one-off. What they do that the painter doesn't yet:

1. **Clothes as a few flat planes with hard edges.** Each garment is two or three flat values (lit, middle, shadow) meeting
   along straight, crisp boundaries, faceted like cut paper. On the figure there is almost no bristle, dry lift or wobble.
   Ours are soft, dry-edged pulls.
2. **The light splits a form with one hard terminator.** The runner's back is two flat values divided by one diagonal edge:
   our G3 pull, but as a plane with a hard edge, not a soft stroke. The broad figure's trousers, the coat and the hoodie all
   turn the same way: a straight cut from light to shadow.
3. **The figure is the crispest thing in the picture.** The city behind is broad flat planes with soft, few marks. The figure
   carries the hard edges and the small accents.
4. **Contact is drawn.**
   - Older vault: both hands flat on the rail, fingers splayed, one on each side; the coat swings out behind as one shape.
   - Child hang: one hand curled over the lip; the knees tucked to the wall, a small gap between the body and the face. This is
     Astra's "exposed interval".
   - Every standing or running figure has a single, crisp, dark cast shadow joined to the feet.
5. **Secondary motion is what makes it move.** The slight figure's scarf and ponytail stream back level, and the coat flares
   behind the vault. The cloth is pushed further than the body.
6. **Accents.** The white shoes and socks are the brightest values on the figure, and the hands are simple clean shapes.
   Faces are near-blank but the hair has a silhouette (tufts, a swept edge).
7. **Silhouette.** Limbs read apart from the torso, and the dark figure sits against a light ground or sky. No outline:
   value contrast does it.
