# Round five: the characters as oil sketches (23 November)

Six characters in the sketch manner on the toned board. The direction now is brushwork and the characters, not the city.
They agree on how a figure is painted:

- **No contour.** The silhouette is where the strokes' broken, dry ends stop.
- **Garments wrapped by a few broad strokes across the form.** The broad figure's green jacket is three or four diagonal sweeps
  round the back. The coat is big diagonals flying out. The runner's back is one blue diagonal. Each is wide (a third of the
  torso or more) and bends round the body; the lit ones lighter and warmer.
- **Limbs: one or two long strokes along them**, a little wider than the limb, ragged at the sides and ends; a bent knee is the
  strokes bending.
- **The skirt: two or three big swirls round the hem**, lifting lighter where the cloth turns up.
- **Hair: a few dark masses, then thin flyaway flicks** off the crown and the ponytail.
- **Socks and white shoes: thick impasto blocks**; dark shoes rounded blobs.
- **Hands: two or three warm touches.** **Packs and bags: two or three strokes of their colour over the figure.**
- **Light:** warm on the lit side, cool violet in the shade, carried by which strokes are lighter, not by gradients.
