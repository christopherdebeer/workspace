# Round three: a target (22 November)

`slight-dive-target.jpg`: the slight figure's dive, painted. It is faithful to the character: lavender top, dark trousers, white
shoes, brown ponytail, the scarf in her ochre accent. It's the target for the figure's painting passes (block-in, modelling,
refinement where attention goes) and for fitted cloth.

What it does:
- **The rig's pose, untouched; the shape all the painter's.** The same skeleton as our dive, but the silhouette is soft cloth over
  a body: the sweater balloons off the back, the trousers are full and bunched, the arm reaches out of a sleeve.
- **Fitted cloth is folds.** Across the trousers, compression folds bunch at the waist and behind the knee, then stack and gather
  at the cuffed ankles. On the sweater, tension pulls run from the armpit along the reaching arm, a fold rolls at the waistband,
  and the cuff gathers at the wrist.
- **Loose cloth flies.** The scarf ends trail back and down; the ponytail whips with loose strands.
- **Modelling by temperature and value, across the form.** Warm lit planes on the shoulder, back and thigh; cool violet half-tones
  as each turns away; a dark accent where folds close. The strokes follow the form's turn, not the bone.
- **Focus.** The sharpest, most worked passages are the face in shadow under the hair, the reaching hand and wrist, the shoes and
  the fold at the hip. Everything else is broader and softer: the back of the legs, the far arm.
- **Edges lost and found.** A hard edge along the top of the back against the wall; soft, lost edges under the body into the
  ground's light. The cast shadow is a separate, soft shape well below.
