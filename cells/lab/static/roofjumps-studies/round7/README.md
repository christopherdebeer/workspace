# Round seven: four studies painted over the round six frames (23 November)

Image-generation studies made on the round six renders, keeping the same poses and cameras:
- `teen-stand-front.jpg`: the teen standing, from the front;
- `slight-stand-front.jpg`: the slight figure standing, from the front;
- `schoolgirl-run.jpg`: the schoolgirl running;
- `older-run-side.jpg`: the older figure running, from the side.

The poses, cameras and palettes are ours, so the gap between these and the renders is the painter's, not the rig's. What they do that the painter doesn't yet:

1. **The garment has its own shape, not the body's.** The hoodie drops off the shoulder, is wider than the chest, blouses over the waistband and gathers at the cuffs. The lavender sleeves balloon into a cuff. The coat is one mass that flares behind the stride, and the skirt flares out from the waist. Our panels follow the rig at a fixed 1.12 puff.
2. **Strokes follow the drape, not the bone.** The hoodie is brushed in diagonals from the shoulder toward the opposite hip, with folds hanging from where the cloth is supported. The trousers are long vertical pulls with one dark plane down the inside of the leg. Ours run along the bone everywhere.
3. **Two or three values a form, split by one edge.** The trousers are a light half and a dark half, meeting along a long edge. The coat has a lit back and a front in shadow. The face is half in light and half in shade. Our per-stroke random value is what makes the cloth stripe.
4. **The face is a few planes.** A band of shadow under the brow (the eye sockets), the side of the nose, a lit cheek and a shaded one, a small mouth. The neck is slender and in shadow, the head small on the body, and the hair has a massed shape with a few loose strands lifting off its edge.
5. **Accents.** The white shoes and socks are the brightest things on each figure, and the sailor collar is one large bright flap with hard edges lying over the shoulders. Hands are simple warm shapes with a darker side, or fists.
6. **Secondary motion carries the run.** The coat streams back, the skirt swings out, and the ponytail flies.
7. **The figure is the crispest thing in the picture.** The city stays broad and soft.
