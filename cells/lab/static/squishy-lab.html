Discovered transitions

Uses the same level generator, collision geometry, movement approximation and Dumpling.step() as the playable Squishy experiment. Moves are discovered by sampling (a missing one is not proof of impossibility), then measured in the player's own units: every flick within ±24 px of drag round the witness, run through the real body, gives where the move goes, the best aim, how far off it may be and still land, and its chance under σ px of drag error. Trials report Wilson 95% intervals. Measure level prices the way home as expected flicks. Flicks played in the game on this device, on this exact level (same fingerprint), are drawn over the maps. Neither WebGL nor the game's renderer is loaded. Read the experiment notes →
