03 / YOUR HAND · choose a card, then a state
Select a card to inspect its routes. Then touch a coloured state to place it.
How to play · what we are exploring

Each state has one routing card. The arrows show the token’s next-state probabilities; four parts always sum to one. A card’s routes are relative to where it is placed: clockwise, across, anticlockwise, or staying put. Select a card in your hand and then a state. That replaces its routes and draws another card. You may place four cards, or release early. A dotted loop means staying in place; it still uses one transition.

The commission is ♣ followed by ♦, starting at ♥. Reaching ♦ before ♣ does not complete it. Once both have been visited, the journey ends. You can undo planning moves, but the result of a released journey is final for this deal. Reset repeats the same deal and random journey, making comparisons possible. The free workshop removes the placement limit and allows repeated journeys.

This is a first rules hypothesis, not a finished game: probability as something you construct together with a card’s visual identity. Should this become a shared competitive network, a solitaire puzzle, or an open-ended probability instrument? Play this small version first. The optional bot duel is a separate first-hit race; no claim of balance yet.
