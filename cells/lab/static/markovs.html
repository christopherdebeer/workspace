03 / YOUR HAND · choose a card, then a space
Use your own die

Roll a real d6 and enter its face; the counter moves by the card it stands on.

How to play
  1. Deal. The four Kings lie at the middles of the edges, the Ace of spades in the centre with the counter on it. The four Twos are shuffled face down and one dealt to each player: its suit is their King, their secret; the rest go back in the box. Three cards each.
  2. Build. In turns, lay one card touching the chain (or anywhere on the rim; an Ace anywhere), turned as you like; arrows needn't line up. Draw back to three. No rolling. Carry on until no space is empty.
  3. Race. Lay one card on top of any junction (or play a Queen to swap two, or pass); then roll. Only the card under the counter decides the move: that face's arrow, one square; off the edge comes in on the far side; a stay face stays. When the counter reaches a King someone holds, that player wins — whoever moved it. Nobody's King sends the counter back to the centre. Thirty moves without an arrival is a draw.
  4. Instead of the die: a Jack walks the counter one square your way; a Joker takes one of the card's exits at random.

Don't say which King is yours. Every card you lay says a little.
