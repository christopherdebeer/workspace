# Piste: the look it's aiming at

`target-1.jpg` … `target-5.jpg`: the reference pictures given at the start (a painting, a black-and-white photograph and
three photographs): a fencer in guard, seen from inside the other's mask, down the piste of a daylit hall; the viewer's
own bell and sleeve in the corner. `node cells/lab/devtools/piste-baseline.mjs` sets the game beside them.

What they show of the kit, piece by piece (the baseline the game's judged against):

- **Mask**: a dark mesh dome, a white bib falling over the jacket's collar.
- **Jacket**: heavy, matte white cotton; soft broad folds, none fine; the sleeve full, gathered at the elbow and into the
  glove's gauntlet; the shoulders square; a high collar under the bib; the hem over the breeches.
- **Glove**: the weapon hand's only, its gauntlet over the sleeve's cuff; the rear hand bare.
- **Breeches**: to just below the knee, roomy at the seat and the thigh, folds at the hip and behind the knee; fastened
  below the knee.
- **Socks**: long, thick, white, ribbed, to over the knee band, slouched a little at the shin.
- **Shoes**: white trainers, low, with a dark stripe.

## The baseline (2026-11-27): the game beside them, `study=near`

Framed alike (the opponent about 2 m off, in guard, then reaching toward you), what differs, biggest first:

1. **The guard itself** (not the kit, but it's most of the picture): theirs is deep and wide, the knees well bent over the
   feet, the back foot set out to the side, the trunk turned a little toward you, the arm reaching with the bell at chest
   height. Ours stands taller and narrower, the feet nearly in line seen from the front, the legs straighter.
2. **Breeches**: theirs full at the seat and the thigh, bunched in folds at the hip and the crotch, banded below the knee.
   Ours are slim, the lofted original (not yet drafted and simulated as the jacket is).
3. **Socks**: theirs thick, ribbed, pulled up over the knee band, a little slack at the shin. Ours thin and smooth.
4. **Shoes**: theirs chunky white trainers with a dark stripe. Ours small, low, a dark sole.
5. **Light**: theirs from the windows, one side of the figure bright, the other in shade, folds read by it; the floor
   shining. Ours flatter and cooler, the figure evenly lit, so heavy cotton's broad folds hardly read.
6. **The jacket**: broadly right now (the shoulders square, the sleeve full); theirs gathers at the elbow and into the
   gauntlet, and stands a little off the body all over. The collar's hidden by the bib in both.
7. **Your own arm**: theirs a sleeve of cloth reaching into the corner, the bell large. Ours similar; the glove reads
   more than the sleeve.

### The guard (2026-11-27)

The back foot's set out to the side now (15 cm off the front's line, from 6), as theirs is. The depth isn't matched yet:
lowered from 0.82 m to 0.80, the hips put the septime lesson on a knife edge (its riposte lands two times in three at most
heights, and moving the lesson's distance either way fails differently), so the deeper knee waits on understanding why.
