# AAOTE: An Agent of the Enemy (v0.5)

A city at night. Everyone at the table is a professional with a private ambition — and one
of you is an agent of the Enemy, building a file of kompromat that must never see daylight.

## Overview

You pursue your ambition through **projects** laid on the table in front of you. A project is
public unless you play it **in stealth** (face down): then everyone sees that you have a hidden
project and how many tokens are on it, but not what it is. Public projects can be helped along
by other players — and sabotaged. Hidden projects are safe from sabotage, but nobody can help
you with what they cannot see, and a table full of hidden projects draws suspicion.

The agent's ambition — **Kompromat** — can only be built in stealth, and only an agent can make
use of it. Find the agent and denounce them before the end of round 8, or the agent walks away
with the file and, in any tie, the win.

## Setup

1. **The Nine Cuts**: each player draws one public power (see Powers).
2. **Ambitions**: deal one secret ambition to each player. Exactly one player is The Enemy's agent.
3. **Hands**: deal 5 cards to each player.

## Gameplay

The game lasts **8 rounds**. On your turn you have **3 actions**:

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Play a project | 1 | Put a project card from your hand on your table, face up — or **in stealth**, face down |
| Progress | 1 | Add a token to one of your own projects; at its required tokens it completes and scores |
| Cooperate | 1 | Add a token to another player's **open** project; you score 1 point for the favour |
| Sabotage | 2 | Clear every token from another player's **open** project |
| Play an event | 1 | Investigate (see a hand), Gossip (see a hidden project), Leak (turn a player's hidden project face up for everyone) — Alibi, held, cancels one aimed at you |
| Draw | 1 | Draw a card (hand limit 7) |
| Denounce | 1 | From round 4, once per game: name the agent — only with evidence |
| Pass | 0 | End your turn |

## Projects

- Small projects need 2 tokens and score 2 points; large projects (Breakthrough, Exposé, Tower,
  Hostile Takeover, Expedition, Thesis) need 3 and score 3. Anyone may complete any project.
- Your **ambition** is three projects of your own kind: completing it scores **4 more**.
- **Kompromat** needs 3 tokens, scores 3, must be played in stealth, and only an agent of the
  Enemy can add tokens to it. Anyone may lay one face down as a decoy.
- A completed hidden project stays hidden. Its points are public; what it was is not.

## Denouncing

From round 4, once per game, you may spend 1 action to name as the agent a player you hold evidence against: Kompromat seen on their table (exposed), in their hand (Investigate), or by Gossip.
- **Right**: the game ends at once. You score 3, every other honest player scores 1, the agent's
  score drops to 0, and the highest score wins.
- **Wrong**: you lose 3 points, your ambition is revealed and no longer counts for you, and your
  turn ends. You keep playing.

Evidence is everywhere if you look: whose projects are hidden, and how many tokens they carry
(a hidden project at three tokens is a large project — or Kompromat); who sabotages whom; what
**Investigate** shows in a hand; what **Gossip** shows you — or what a **Leak** shows everyone. Exposing the
agent helps everyone a little and the leader most — so who pays for the investigation?

## Powers (public)

- **Rin** (power over others): sabotage costs 1 action.
- **Jin** (knowing the thoughts of others): once per game, see a player's ambition.
- **Tō** (harmony): cooperating costs nothing.
- **Kai** (premonition of danger): once per game, see a player's ambition (a second Jin).
- **Zen** (enlightenment): you always see the top card of the deck.

## Winning

The game ends when someone denounces the agent correctly, or at the end of round 8. The
highest score wins. The agent wins any tie.

## Strategy notes

- Honest players: your ambition is worth 10 points over the game; a correct denunciation is
  worth 3 to you and 1 to everyone else — and takes the agent out. Cooperate with the player you
  trust, and expose the hidden project you don't.
- The agent: look busy. Build an open project or two, help a neighbour, and keep the file moving
  only when nobody is watching. A hidden project at three tokens that never turns face up is
  the one thing you cannot explain.
