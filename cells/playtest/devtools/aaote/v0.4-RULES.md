# AAOTE: An Agent of the Enemy (v0.4)

A game of hidden objectives on a map you build together — and one traitor among you.

## Overview

Everyone has a secret objective. Four are honest goals: collect, explore, build, trade. One player is **The Enemy**, who wins by gathering the three Forbidden Items hidden around the map — or simply by surviving to the end of round 10 unexposed. Everyone else races their own goal while watching for the traitor: at any time from round 3 you may **denounce** the player you suspect. Be right and you win on the spot. Be wrong and you are finished.

## Setup

1. **Player cards**: each player gets one public ability at random (see Player Cards).
2. **Objectives**: deal one secret objective to each player. Exactly one player is The Enemy.
3. **Map**: only the Origin exists. All players start there.
4. **Hands**: deal 5 cards to each player from the main deck.

## Gameplay

You have **3 action points (AP)** each turn:

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Place location | 1 AP | Put a location card from your hand next to a location with an open side |
| Move | 1 AP | Step to a linked location |
| Search | 1 AP | Take an item lying where you stand |
| Play event | 1 AP | Play an event card |
| Trade offer | 1 AP | Offer one of your items to a player; they give one back or decline (one offer per turn) |
| Draw | 1 AP | Draw a card (hand limit 7) |
| Denounce | 1 AP | From round 3, once per game: name The Enemy |
| Pass | 0 AP | End your turn |

## The map

- Locations link to the location they were placed next to; each location has at most 4 links. You move one link at a time.
- **Discovery**: the first time you enter any location, draw a card — exploring is how you find things.
- Mountain Passes need a **Rope** in hand to enter; the Hidden Cave needs a **Lantern**.
- **Forbidden Items lie on the map**: the Cursed Amulet in the (first) Ancient Ruins, the Dark Tome in the Hidden Cave, the Shadow Key in the Forbidden Temple. Anyone standing there may **search** to take one. Honest players can take them too — to keep them from The Enemy.
- The **Forbidden Temple** admits only The Enemy, and entering it reveals them to everyone.
- The Hidden Cave hides your position; from the Watchtower you see everyone's.

## Objectives

- **The Collector**: hold 5 different items at once.
- **The Explorer**: visit 6 different locations.
- **The Builder**: place 5 location cards.
- **The Trader**: complete 4 trades, with at least 3 different players.
- **The Enemy**: hold the 3 Forbidden Items at once — or reach the end of round 10 without being denounced.

You win the moment your objective is met.

## Denouncing

From round 3, once per game, you may spend 1 AP to name the player you believe is The Enemy.
- **Right**: you win immediately.
- **Wrong**: your objective is revealed to everyone, it no longer counts for you (you can't win), and your turn ends. You keep playing — you can still hinder The Enemy.

Information is the road to a safe denunciation: **Spy** shows a hand, **Interrogate** (spend a Supplies) and 3 **Map Fragments** show an objective, the Mystic sees one objective, and the Watchtower shows where everyone is. Who is searching for Forbidden Items? Who took the Dark Tome — and who wants it?

## Trading

- One offer per turn: offer one item to a player; they choose one of their items to give back, or decline.
- The Merchant's offers cost 0 AP; so do offers made from a Village Square.

## Player cards (public)

- **The Scholar** sees the top card of the deck.
- **The Merchant** makes trade offers for 0 AP.
- **The Scout** moves up to 2 steps for 1 AP.
- **The Guardian** cannot be targeted by Theft or Spy.
- **The Mystic** may once see one player's objective.

## Events

Swift Journey (2 free moves), Shortcut (move next to any player), Spy, Interrogate, Roadblock (block a location until the end of next round), Theft (take a random item from a player on or next to your location), Sabotage (remove an empty location), Evasion (held: cancels the next event aimed at you), Hidden Path (move up to 2 steps, hidden).

## Winning

The game ends when someone completes their objective, when someone denounces The Enemy correctly, or at the end of round 10 — then The Enemy wins.

## Strategy notes

- Honest players: your own race matters, but so does the traitor. Denying the Forbidden Items and gathering evidence can win as surely as your objective.
- The Enemy: look busy with an honest-seeming goal. Gather the Amulet and the Tome quietly; the Temple is your last step — enter and take the Key in one turn, and win before anyone can answer.
